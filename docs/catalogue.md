# Catalogue: where every result comes from

Generated by `python scripts/run.py sources.catalogue` from `src/dgt_stats/catalogue.py`; do not edit by hand.

The study is organised in five sections. Each has its own sources, its own stages in the pipeline (`python scripts/run.py <section>`), its own code (`src/dgt_stats/<section>/`), result tables (`reports/tables/<section>/`), documents (`docs/<section>/`) and pages on the site. No record is linked across sources; where two sources meet, it is at an aggregate both genuinely have (province and year) or as a model trained on one and tested on the other's records.

Below the summary, each section lists its stages and every one of its result tables, with the sources the table is computed from, the stage that writes it and the site pages that publish it; [the last part](#sources-list) lists every source with its files. To find where a figure comes from, search this page for its table or its source.
