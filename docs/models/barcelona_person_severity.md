# Model card: barcelona person severity

Generated by `python scripts/microdata.py validate`; do not edit by hand.

## Task

Among people recorded in Barcelona crashes, which observed circumstances and road-user characteristics are most predictive of a serious or fatal injury?

- **Observation (one row):** one person record in a Barcelona crash, 2025.
- **Target:** `serious_or_fatal` = 1 if Descripcio_victimitzacio is 'Ferit greu' or 'Mort' (within or after 24 h), 0 if 'Il.lès' or 'Ferit lleu' (any of three); records with a blank victimisation or 'Mort natural' are excluded.
- **Feature table:** `data/features/barcelona_person_severity.parquet` (15,848 rows, id `person_record_id`, grouped by `Numero_expedient`).
- **Source files:** `data/raw/barcelona/2025/accidents_gu_bcn_2025.csv` (SHA-256 `2bd4dc9f35e1…`); `data/raw/barcelona/2025/accidents_tipus_gu_bcn_2025.csv` (SHA-256 `be50665fef12…`); `data/raw/barcelona/2025/accidents_causes_mediates_gu_bcn_2025.csv` (SHA-256 `17d6df8b5dd5…`); `data/raw/barcelona/2025/accidents_causa_conductor_gu_bcn_2025.csv` (SHA-256 `2d0f89d743d1…`); `data/raw/barcelona/2025/accidents_persones_gu_bcn_2025.csv` (SHA-256 `a851a51b2478…`); `data/raw/barcelona/2025/accidents_vehicles_gu_bcn_2025.csv` (SHA-256 `6f50be9587d4…`).
- **Layer:** Rich microdata: Barcelona.
- **Decision:** KEEP but ranking-only (gain +0.063 (+0.020 to +0.106) over the table: adds signal).
- **What it does:** ranks the people recorded in crashes by how likely they were seriously or fatally injured, from road role, vehicle, age, sex and crash circumstances.
- **Not answered:** anyone's risk per trip; the behaviour of a person (causes have no person key); other cities or years.

## Data and split

- Design: train months 1-9 with 5-fold cross-validation grouped by crash; test months 10-12.
- Training rows (final fit): 11,799, of which 202 positive.
- Test rows: 4,049, of which 58 positive (prevalence 1.4%).
- Isolation: ids shared between training and test: 0; crashes shared: 0; crashes shared across cross-validation folds: 0.
- Model choice (estimator and its two-point grid) is made on validation data only; the test rows are used once, for the numbers below.

## Valid interpretation

- Identifying which recorded road-user groups (role, associated vehicle type, age) and crash types concentrate serious and fatal injuries among people involved in Barcelona crashes in 2025.
- Ranking person profiles for road-safety analysis of vulnerable users; probabilities are shown only as a ranking when calibration fails.

## Invalid interpretation

- It is not a causal model: a feature that the model uses is associated with the outcome in these records; changing it is not shown to change the outcome.
- It does not predict whether a crash will happen, or anyone's risk per trip, per kilometre or per person: there is no exposure in these data.
- It is not a prediction about an individual driver, and recorded causes are not findings about a person (the cause tables have no person key).
- It does not say how many deaths or injuries any measure would prevent.

## Limitations

- One year (2025) of one city; the test months are the last three of the same year.
- Few positive cases: every count of serious or fatal injuries is in the tables, and the intervals are wide.
- Person records without a recorded victimisation (mostly drivers whose age and sex are also missing) and one natural death are excluded from the target.
- The vehicle type on a pedestrian's record does not say which vehicle it is; crash-level recorded causes are crash context, never the person's own behaviour.
