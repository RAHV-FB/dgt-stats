Withdrawn analysis

Deaths attributed to distraction

This analysis was withdrawn because its results came from coefficients published in external studies rather than from data in this repository.

This page estimated how many deaths a year distraction causes, by combining the share of fatal crashes in which the police recorded distraction with a crash risk measured in a driving study in the United States. The repository holds no Spanish data on how much distraction raises the risk of a crash.

What the repository's own data show on this subject is on these pages: Recorded factors, how often the police record alcohol, distraction and other factors in crashes. The methodology explains how the results on the site are produced.

Road safety in Spain, an independent analysis by Russell Howard (RAHV-FB). Data from the Dirección General de Tráfico, INE, the Ministerio de Transportes, CORES, the Servei Català de Trànsit, the Ajuntament de Barcelona, the Autoritat del Transport Metropolità (EMEF) and the Consorcio Regional de Transportes de Madrid (Powered by CRTM). All results are computed from the published files by the code in the repository.
