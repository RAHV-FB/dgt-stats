Crashes and casualties in Barcelona, 2025

Every crash Barcelona's city police attended in 2025, with each person involved: who was seriously hurt, in which kinds of crash, and the causes the police recorded.

The Guàrdia Urbana, Barcelona's city police, recorded 7,741 crashes in 2025, with every person involved. Among the 15,848 people whose outcome was recorded, 1.6% were seriously or fatally injured. Pedestrians and motorcyclists were much more often seriously or fatally injured than car drivers: 6.5% of pedestrians and 3.3% of motorcyclists, against 3 of 4,671 car drivers (0.06%). These shares are conditional on appearing in a recorded crash. The data contain no measure of how much each group travels, so they are not rates per journey or per kilometre.

Unlike the Catalan file, the records include crashes without a serious injury and people who were not hurt. People whose outcome was not recorded, and one death the police recorded as natural, are left out of the shares. The crash, person, vehicle and cause tables are linked by a crash number but share no identifier with the Catalan file or DGT's records.

Serious injury by road user, age and sex

The shares for cyclists (1.3%), moped riders (2.2%) and personal mobility vehicle riders (2.1%) lie between those of car drivers and motorcyclists. By age, the share is highest among people aged 75 and over (5.3%); in every younger age band it lies between 0.9% and 2.1%. Women and men differ little (1.8% and 1.6%).

Serious injury by type of crash

Of the 7,741 crashes, 3.3% had at least one serious or fatal injury, more than the share of people because one seriously injured person is enough to place a crash in that group. When a pedestrian was struck, the share was 7.3%, more than twice the average. The highest share, 60.5% of 43 crashes, belongs to a category the Guàrdia Urbana codes as a rear collision while catching up; of the 1,672 crashes it codes as rear-end collisions, 0.1% had a serious or fatal injury. A model of these crashes ranked them no better than this grouping by accident type (predictive models).

Causes recorded by the police

For each crash the Guàrdia Urbana records one or more causes or contributing factors, such as lack of attention, alcohol or the state of the road. They are classifications the police make after attending the crash, a crash can have several, and they are not causal estimates produced by this study. They are also recorded for the crash as a whole: no key links a cause to the driver or vehicle concerned, so a crash with alcohol recorded cannot be tied to the person who had been drinking, or to the person who was hurt.

The cause recorded most often is lack of attention to driving (2,082 crashes). Among causes recorded in at least 30 crashes, a serious or fatal injury was most often recorded where excessive or inappropriate speed was: 18.4% of 38 crashes (95% interval 9.2–33.4%), against 3.3% of all crashes. Alcohol was recorded in 337 crashes, 47.5% of them at night, against 9.3% of the other crashes.
