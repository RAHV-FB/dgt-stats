Pedestrians, cyclists, moped riders, motorcyclists and personal-mobility-vehicle users are the vulnerable road users: they have no protective shell. Their share of all deaths is far higher on urban streets than on interurban roads.

Drivers, by vehicle, since 1993

Car-driver deaths fell by 77% between 1993 and 2013 and have been flat since (495 in 2024). Motorcycle-rider deaths did not follow: they rose through the 2000s, dipped, and are back at 415 in 2024, above their 1997–1999 average of 371 and close to the number of car drivers killed.
