Spain

Seasonal variation in road deaths

Monthly road deaths in Spain from 2014 to 2024, set beside sales of road fuel and petrol and traffic on the toll motorways, with the 2020 lockdown treated separately.

Road deaths in Spain peak in summer. Compared with an average month of the same year, July has 1.22 times the deaths and August 1.14 times. More road fuel is sold in those months, and part of the excess goes with it. Per tonne of road fuel sold, the one monthly series that covers every road and every vehicle, July and August stand at 1.12× and 1.09×: smaller, but with 95% intervals that lie wholly above the average month. On the same basis February to May are below the average month and September above it. In April 2020, under the lockdown, deaths fell 68% and road fuel sold 58% against the average April of 2017–2019.

Deaths, fuel sales and motorway traffic by month

No series counts the kilometres driven on all Spanish roads month by month. Road fuel sold (petrol plus diesel) is the one series used to divide deaths; as on the annual pages, a rate per tonne of fuel stands in for a rate per kilometre (Trends since 2019). Two narrower series are shown beside deaths only: petrol sold, which leaves out every diesel vehicle, and traffic on the state toll motorways, measured directly but on a small part of the network, as vehicles per kilometre of motorway. The three series diverge in summer. In the average August, deaths stand at 113 on this index, road fuel sold at 105 (down from 109 in July), petrol sold at 116 (up from 115) and toll-motorway traffic at 140. Which of them comes closest to the kilometres driven on all roads is unknown.

Each month against the average month

Each month is compared with the average month of the same year, so the long-run trend does not enter the seasonal pattern; a second version divides deaths by road fuel sold. The intervals allow for monthly counts that vary more than chance alone would produce. Both versions pool nine years from 2014 to 2024, leaving out 2020 and 2021.

Per tonne of road fuel, the 95% intervals for July (1.06–1.18) and August (1.03–1.15) lie above 1. February to May lie between 0.90× and 0.92×, with intervals below 1; September, at 1.10×, has an interval above 1. The intervals of the other months include the average month.

The 2020 lockdown

April 2020 was the one full month under the strictest restrictions. Against the average April of 2017–2019, deaths fell 68%, road fuel sold 58%, petrol sold 76% and toll-motorway traffic 78%. Deaths therefore fell by more than road fuel and by less than the two narrower series, and per tonne of road fuel they fell 24%.

Both results depend on the measure set against deaths. Against road fuel sold, deaths rise more than fuel sales in summer and fell more than fuel sales in the April lockdown; against petrol sales or toll-motorway traffic, the reverse. None of these series measures kilometres driven on all roads, so how much of the summer excess would remain per kilometre is unknown.
