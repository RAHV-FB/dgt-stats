Timing

When crashes happen and when they turn deadly: by hour, weekday, road type and lighting, from 875,013 injury crashes recorded between 2016 and 2024.

Hour and weekday

Crashes cluster in the afternoon rush on weekdays. Fatal outcomes follow a different clock: the share of crashes that kill someone is lowest in the daytime and peaks between 03:00 and 05:59 in the morning on every day of the week. The microdata carry neither traffic volumes nor speeds, so the reason is not observable here. The single highest cells are early on Wednesday, Tuesday and Monday, but they hold about 500 crashes each, around 29 of them fatal, so the differences between the cells at the top are not resolvable; pooled over 00:00–06:59, weekend nights (3.5%) are slightly deadlier than weekday nights (3.2%).

Darkness

Darkness holds 25% of interurban injury crashes in 2024 but 34% of interurban deaths; on urban streets the two are 21% and 27%.
