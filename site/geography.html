Provinces

The rate is deaths in crashes that happened in the province divided by the people who live there, so provinces crossed by long-distance traffic (Zamora, Soria, Cuenca, Huesca) rank high partly because many of the dead were passing through, and dense urban provinces rank low because most travel there is slow and short. The intervals are wide for the small provinces: one crash more or less moves Soria or Teruel by several places, so the ranking should be read in groups, not position by position.

Spain over time

Per resident, deaths fell by almost three quarters between 2002 and 2013 and have been flat since. Per licence holder the line is flat too: the census has grown by about the same share as the population, so neither denominator changes the story of the last decade.
