Trends and rates explorer

Spain's road deaths, hospital admissions and injury crashes year by year, as counts or as rates, for the period you choose.

Indicator
Period

The figures year by year

This tool needs JavaScript. The results it draws on are on the pages linked below.

Every figure is a count or a ratio of counts published by DGT, INE, CORES and the Ministerio de Transportes; a rate is shown only for the years its denominator exists, and no year is filled in. Comparing two indicators shows each as an index, the first year of the period being 100.

The long-run trend and how far recent years depart from it are on the long-run trends page; definitions are on the methodology page.

Road safety in Spain, an independent analysis by Russell Howard (RAHV-FB). Data from the Dirección General de Tráfico, INE, the Ministerio de Transportes, CORES, the Servei Català de Trànsit, the Ajuntament de Barcelona, the Autoritat del Transport Metropolità, Idescat and Institut Metròpoli (EMEF), Idescat (population census) and the Consorcio Regional de Transportes de Madrid (Powered by CRTM). All results are computed by the code in the repository from the published files and the cited values it holds.
