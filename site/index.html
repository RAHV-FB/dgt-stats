What the data say

  • Trends: 1,785 deaths in 2024, 72% fewer than in 1993; the fall stopped around 2013.
  • Timing: Night hours hold 25% of interurban crashes but 34% of interurban deaths (2024).
  • Road users: Pedestrians, cyclists, moped, motorcycle and scooter users are 79% of urban deaths and 40% of interurban ones (2024).
  • Geography: Deaths per 100,000 residents run from 1.2 in Melilla to 14.5 in Zamora (2024), with intervals wide enough that most provinces overlap.
  • Older drivers: Drivers aged 75 and over die 0.74 times as often as drivers aged 35–64 per resident, 1.55 times per licence holder and 3.0 times per driver involved in a crash: the denominator decides the answer.
  • Severity: Given an injury crash, the crash type 'pedestrian struck' carries 6.4 times the odds of a death of the reference type; a model fitted to 2016–2022 scores 2023–2024 with an area under the curve of 0.80.
  • Vehicles per km: A heavy truck is in a fatal crash 10 times as often as a car per registered vehicle but 2.5 times per kilometre driven (2022); most of the people killed are outside the truck.
  • Policy: The July 2006 points licence coincided with a 12% drop in monthly deaths beyond the trend, larger than any of the 38 placebo breaks (rank 1); the 2019 speed limit shows a change a placebo break in January 2018 reproduces (-12%), so no claim is made.
  • Speed: DGT's report records inappropriate speed in 7% of injury crashes (2023, without Cataluña and País Vasco); in the yearbook's driver tables 52% of drivers have no speed record at all.

How to read the numbers

Counts are DGT's consolidated figures: an injury crash is one with at least one person killed or injured, and deaths are counted within 30 days of the crash unless a chart says otherwise. Crash counts describe what the police recorded, not the risk of travelling; rates on the trends page divide by the vehicle fleet or the population.

Built from the Dirección General de Tráfico (DGT) open data: crash microdata 2016–2024, the historical series of the Anuario de Accidentes 2024, the 2024 statistical tables and the driver census. Every number is reproducible from the repository; see the data page for the checks.
